# Bayanihan: Disaster Rescue Game

A flood-rescue game by **ELEV8 GAMES** (capstone project). A typhoon has flooded the barangay: take the rescue boat out,
find the residents who are stranded, treat and tow them, and bring them to the evacuation hall to be registered before
time runs out. The main map is the real **Barangay Mabuhay, Carmona, Cavite**, built from OpenStreetMap with its
landmarks. Phone first and landscape, with touch controls; keyboard and mouse are supported.

*Bayanihan*: when a community lifts together, no one is left behind.

## Requirements
- Unity Editor **6000.3.9f1** (URP 17.3, Cinemachine 3.1, Timeline 1.8, Input System 1.18, glTFast)
- Git LFS (audio, models and textures are stored with it)

## Open and play
1. `git lfs install`, then clone the repository.
2. In Unity Hub choose **Add project from disk** and pick this folder; open it with Unity 6000.3.9f1.
3. Open **Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity** and press Play (or start from `Account.unity` to sign in first).

The scenes are in Build Settings in this order: `Account`, `MainMenu`, `Loadout`, `Maps/MabuhayGameplay`, `Gameplay`
(the Manila sample map), and the two cutscene scenes, which load on top of the menu and the mission.

## How a mission goes
1. **Main menu**: a looping storm over Mabuhay plays behind the menu.
2. **Loadout**: pick the map and pack 4 of the 6 items (Floatie, First-Aid Kit, Flashlight, Rope, Radio, Raincoat)
   onto the rescuer's dry-bag. LAUNCH zips it up and plays a short READY.
3. **Flood intro** (Mabuhay, can be turned off): 24 seconds of the rain, the river, the drains and the streets filling,
   then the barangay's response. SKIP at any time.
4. **The mission**: the rescuer starts at the helm. Ten residents are in the water, on posts, on roofs or behind debris;
   triage tags show who is most urgent. Live wires, floating wreckage and rising water are the hazards.
5. **The report**: who was saved, in what order, and the score.

### Controls
| Keyboard and mouse | Touch | |
|---|---|---|
| W A S D / arrows | Joystick | Steer the boat, or swim |
| Mouse, scroll | Drag, pinch | Look around, zoom |
| E (hold to treat) | RESCUE | Pull someone aboard, board or leave the boat |
| T | THROW | Throw the Floatie or Rope |
| F / Q / C | LIGHT / RADIO / POWER CUT | Flashlight, find the nearest resident, have a live wire cut |
| V | | Swimmer's view: first or third person |
| Esc | Pause button | Pause, restart, settings, abort; skips a cutscene |

### Settings
Master, music and sound-effect volumes; graphics (Low, Medium, High); colour grade; look speed; swim view; touch
controls and joystick side; **Play Intro**, **Reduce Motion** (no camera shake or lightning flashes, a still menu
background) and **Subtitles**.

## Building the generated content
The Mabuhay map and the cutscenes are **generated by editor builders**, not edited by hand. Change the builder, then run
it from the Unity menu:
- **Bayanihan > Build Mabuhay Gameplay Scene**: the whole map (see [Rebuilding Mabuhay](Docs/Workflows/Rebuilding%20Mabuhay.md)).
- **Bayanihan > Cutscenes > Build Audio / Build Intro / Build Menu Cinematic**: the cinematics.

## Tests
Open **Window > General > Test Runner**: about 270 EditMode and 230 PlayMode tests cover the rescue rules, the map,
the boat and swimmer, the UI and the cinematics. See [Running Tests](Docs/Workflows/Running%20Tests.md).
